# Glintbox — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

Glintbox ("the app") is a status keepsake app for Android. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Summary

Glintbox **does not collect, store, transmit, share or sell any personal data**. The app has **no internet permission**, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers.

## Information we collect

None. App preferences (theme, accent palette, the folders you chose, toggles) are stored only on your device in the app's private storage and are removed when you uninstall the app. They are excluded from cloud backup.

## Files on your device

With your explicit permission, Glintbox:

- **reads** media files from the status folder you grant (on Android 11+ through the system folder picker; on Android 8–10 through the standard storage permission), and
- **writes** copies of the files you choose to the save folder you select (by default `Internal storage/WhatsApp Statuses`).

All processing happens on your device. Files never leave your device unless you explicitly tap **Share** or **Repost**, in which case Android passes the file to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

You can revoke folder access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Glintbox, or by uninstalling the app.

## Optional auto-save

If you turn on Auto-save, the app periodically copies new statuses from the granted folder to your save folder using Android's WorkManager. This runs entirely on the device.

## Children

Glintbox is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
